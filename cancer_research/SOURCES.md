## Known gaps

- **Fetch failures.** `fda.gov` "Resources Information Approved Drugs" pages returned 403/404 to automated fetching, and `onclive.com` returned 403. Approval facts were confirmed through company IR releases and trade press instead. Rows citing a generic `fda.gov` landing URL are historical approvals (pre-2025) drawn from general knowledge, not re-verified individually this session — they are well-established and low-risk, but the URL is a section pointer rather than a specific announcement.
- **Not covered.** EU/EMA and China NMPA regulatory status (except where noted for camizestrant); pricing and reimbursement; phase 1 assets; radiopharmaceuticals; supportive care.
- **Sequencing evidence is thin.** With four approved oral SERDs, three TROP2 ADCs, and overlapping ADC indications, almost no head-to-head trials exist to guide sequencing. Statements about competitive position are about label overlap, not proven comparative efficacy.
